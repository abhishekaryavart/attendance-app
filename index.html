The Solution

How IndicPro Works

A seamless connection between your school's desktop and the teacher's mobile device. Authorized staff displays a dynamic QR code, and teachers simply scan it.

  • QR Staff opens the QR page via Desktop Portal
  • System generates a fast, Dynamic QR
  • Teachers scan using the IndicPro Mobile App
  • Backend securely validates identity & location
  • Attendance accurately marked & reported in real-time
Secure & Reliable

Next-Gen Dynamic QR Security

Our QR codes aren't just static images. They change dynamically, ensuring proxy attendance is completely blocked.

  • Short Validity: QR code expires quickly, refreshing automatically
  • Server Validation: Deep cryptographic validation on the server
  • Expired QR Rejection: No one can scan a photo of an old QR
  • Protected Records: Secure login with strict access roles
